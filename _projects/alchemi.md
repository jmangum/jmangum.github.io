<div><h2>The ALma Comprehensive High-resolution Extragalactic Molecular Inventory (ALCHEMI)</h2></div>
<p>ALCHEMI was an ALMA Cycle 5 Large Programme (ALMA project 2017.1.00161.L) designed to image the circumnuclear zone (CMZ: 850x340 pc) of the nearby starburst galaxy NGC253 from 84 to 163 GHz (Bands 3 and 4) and from 211 to 373 GHz (Bands 6 and 7) at 1.6 arcsec (27pc) spatial resolution. An ALMA Cycle 6 project was added to ALCHEMI to augment its capabilities with 1.6 arcsec imaging of the NGC253 CMZ at ALMA Band 5 (163 to 211 GHz: ALMA Project 2018.1.00162.S).  Further details on this project can be found on the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/alchemi">ALCHEMI web page</a>.  A complete listing of publications derived from the ALCHEMI survey is provided below.</p>
