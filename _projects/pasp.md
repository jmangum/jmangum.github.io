<p>I have been the Editor-in-Chief for the Publications of the Astronomical Society of the Pacific (PASP) since 2013.  PASP publishes high-quality astrophysical research results from a very broad range of topics.  Details regarding PASP's scope, including information for prospective authors, can be found on <a href="https://iopscience.iop.org/journal/1538-3873">PASP's Institute of Physics Publishing (IOPP) web pages</a>.</p>

<p>We are particularly interested in publishing reviews, tutorials, and focus issues which strive to educate researchers on a wide range of topics.  For examples of the tutorial, review, and focus issue content that we publish, see our <a href="https://iopscience.iop.org/journal/1538-3873/page/journal-collections">collections pages</a>.  If you are interested in writing a review, tutorial, or would like organize a focus issue for PASP, please do let me know.</p>
