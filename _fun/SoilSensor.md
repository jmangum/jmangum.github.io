<div><h3>Soil Moisture Sensor with a Cool Neopixel Display</h3></div><br>

<p>Built this soil moisture sensor for my wife as a high-tech replacement for one of those resistance-sensing soil moisture sensors that you can buy at hardware stores.  Uses an microcontroller connected to a Neopixel panel that I have programmed (using <a href="https://circuitpython.org">CircuitPython</a>) to track moisture content.  You can see it in action in the movie below.  I will post the design to Adafruit Playground at some point.</p>
