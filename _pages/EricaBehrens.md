<h2>Erica Behrens</h2>
Erica earned her PhD in Astronomy from the University of Virginia in April 2026 and is now a postdoctoral researcher in Eva Schinnerer's research group at the Max Planck Institute for Astronomy in Heidelberg, Germany.  I have been extremely fortunate to work with Erica over the past six-plus years on a wide-range of studies of molecular spectral line and continuum imaging of the star formation process in galaxies.
